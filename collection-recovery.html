Revenue Recovery Architecture

The 4 Capital Recovery Disciplines

Stopping cash leakage across delinquent balances, unpaid tolls, municipal citations, police impounds, and vehicle damage subrogation.

1. Delinquent Balance Recovery

Systematic audit of negative ledger balances. Executing automated card re-runs, scheduled payment arrangements, and structured debtor communication to reclaim unpaid extension days.

2. Automated Toll & Citation Matching

Reconciling FasTrak, E-ZPass, and municipal parking citations against exact contract check-out/check-in timestamps. Automatically attaching authorized administrative fees.

3. Police Impound & Tow Subrogation

Immediate coordination with municipal impound yards. Disputing unlawful administrative hold fees, recovering vehicles within hours to halt daily storage fees, and billing the at-fault renter.

4. Forensic Insurance Dossiers

Compiling indisputable forensic claim packages: signed rental contracts, date-stamped walkaround photos, damage appraisal estimates, and telematics logs submitted directly to primary insurers.
